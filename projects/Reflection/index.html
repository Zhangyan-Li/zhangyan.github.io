Looking back at Walk, Recipe, and Visual Only, I realized I keep coming back to the same things: color, type, and visual effects. Each project used them differently. Walk was about letting people get a feel for a place by clicking and moving through it. In Recipe, I played around a lot with typography, gradients, and glow effects, mostly because I wanted the page to feel alive instead of flat. Visual Only was the most fun for me, since I didn’t have to lean on text and could just focus on how things looked. The projects weren’t trying to do the same thing, but I noticed I always wanted the site to do something, not just sit there. I also picked up a lot more CSS along the way. It surprised me how much a small tweak to spacing, a color, or an animation could change how the whole page felt. Next, I want to try more complicated interactions and figure out how to make the visuals and the interaction work together, instead of treating them as two separate things.
